# Commerce Revenue Agent — Product North Star

## Category
Commerce revenue intelligence and prospecting orchestration for ecommerce agencies, consultancies, app/service vendors, and commerce teams.

## Core promise
Tell the user **who to pursue now, why the account matters, who owns the problem, what evidence supports the opportunity, and what the recommended next action is.**

## Primary user
Initial ICP:
- Shopify / ecommerce agencies
- CRO agencies
- retention/Klaviyo agencies
- ecommerce consultancies
- Shopify app/service vendors

DCL is customer #1.

## Problems we solve
Existing GTM tools are often strong at data, sequencing or broad intent, but users still have to determine whether an account is genuinely relevant and why. Commerce service sellers especially need domain-specific intelligence that generic contact databases do not provide.

The product combines:
- account fit
- storefront/site intelligence
- commerce technology
- hiring/leadership/growth signals
- service-to-problem matching
- buyer mapping
- verified contact data
- evidence-grounded outreach
- pipeline and CRM context

## Product model
Every account has separate:
- Fit Score
- Need Score
- Intent Score
- Opportunity Score

A score without evidence is invalid.

## Signature experiences
### Daily Command Center
A concise list of what deserves attention today: newly urgent accounts, changed accounts, warm reactivations, follow-ups, replies and meetings.

### Opportunity Page
One narrative view containing:
- why now
- commercial thesis
- fit/need/intent scores
- detected commerce opportunities
- signal timeline
- buyer map
- verified contacts
- recommended motion
- approved proof/case study
- draft outreach
- source receipts

### Signal Timeline
Show material changes over time, not just current attributes.

### Commerce Audit
Public storefront intelligence that identifies cautious, evidence-backed opportunities in CRO, UX, subscriptions, merchandising, retention, performance and commerce technology.

### Buyer Map
Ranked decision makers based on the actual problem. Do not simply return the most senior person.

### Native Pipeline + CRM Sync
Native stages: New → Qualified → Ready → Contacted → Engaged → Meeting → Opportunity → Proposal → Won/Lost.
External CRM integrations should eventually include HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio and Pipedrive with suppression and activity/deal sync.

## Differentiation
Do not compete primarily on having the largest contact database or the highest sending volume. Win on:
1. Commerce-specific understanding.
2. Explainable evidence and scoring.
3. Superior prioritization — fewer, better opportunities.
4. Research-to-action workflow with minimal clicks.
5. Signal/change detection over time.
6. Trust: source receipts, dates and confidence.
7. Ability to work as a complete system or integrate with the customer's existing CRM.

## Hard product rules
- No reason to contact → no outreach draft.
- No verified professional email → not outreach-ready.
- No unsupported claims in drafts.
- Generic storefront emails are not decision-maker contacts.
- Technology presence does not automatically imply a problem.
- AI does not directly choose opaque scores.
- Human approval required for outreach in the initial commercial release.

## Success metrics
North-star: qualified opportunities and meetings that users say they would not have identified as quickly without the product.

Supporting metrics:
- % surfaced accounts accepted as worth pursuing
- positive reply rate
- qualified meeting rate
- opportunity creation rate
- cost per qualified opportunity
- time from discovery to approved outreach
- contact verification rate
- false-positive signal rate
- suppression/duplicate error rate

## Initial quality gate
Before autonomous sending is considered, a 50-account DCL validation run should achieve at least 70% "yes, worth pursuing" judgment from the operator.
