# DCL Revenue Agent — M0 product specification v0.1

## Objective
Build an owned revenue-intelligence platform that discovers Shopify merchants DCL should pursue, identifies evidence-backed reasons to contact them, maps the likely buyer, and eventually supports controlled outreach and follow-up.

## Non-goals
- Bulk list blasting
- Guessing contact data
- Auto-sending commercial replies in early versions
- Treating every Shopify store as a lead
- Depending on one data/enrichment provider

## Account model
Every account has three independent scores:
1. Fit — whether this is the type of company DCL wants.
2. Need — whether DCL can identify a material problem/opportunity.
3. Intent — whether there is evidence of near-term investment/change.

Opportunity = 40% Fit + 30% Need + 30% Intent, multiplied by evidence confidence. Intent is deliberately weighted equally with Need so fresh commercial signals materially change prioritization.

## Signal families
Hiring, leadership, storefront, technology, growth/company events, account-fit discovery.

## Outreach gate
No email can be generated until all are true:
- Fit score >= 55
- There is a source-backed commercial thesis
- A relevant buyer is identified
- Contact information is verified or user-supplied
- Every claimed DCL proof point is approved
- Account is not suppressed

## Initial sales motions
- growth-partner
- specialist-gap
- extend-internal-team
- bridge-while-hiring
- research-first

## M1 acceptance criteria
Produce 50 real, qualified Shopify accounts. DCL should judge at least 70% as genuinely worth pursuing. If not, improve discovery/scoring before adding email automation.
