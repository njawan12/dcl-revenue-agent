# Competitive Landscape and Gap Thesis

## Products to benchmark
- Apollo — contact data, sequencing, CRM workflows, broad GTM platform
- Clay — enrichment, waterfalls, flexible workflows, agents, observability
- Common Room — intent/signals and account context
- 11x / Artisan — autonomous SDR workflows
- Smartlead / Instantly — outbound infrastructure and deliverability
- Unify / Amplemarket — trigger-to-sequence workflows and prospecting

## Table stakes we must meet
- good account/contact search and filtering
- verified professional contacts
- explainable enrichment status
- saved views / prioritization
- outreach drafts and sequence state
- CRM-aware suppression and duplicate protection
- activity history
- integrations and/or credible native pipeline
- fast onboarding and low-friction configuration
- strong empty/error/provider-failure states

## Gaps we should exploit
### 1. Commerce-specific understanding
Generic GTM systems know companies and people. We should understand the commerce storefront itself: Shopify/Plus, PDP/collection UX, subscriptions, retention stack, merchandising, app/technology signals, performance and ecommerce-specific hiring/leadership context.

### 2. Better prioritization, not more rows
The product should default to a small number of accounts worth acting on now, with reasons. Avoid making users sift through thousands of leads.

### 3. Evidence receipts
Every meaningful opportunity claim should be inspectable by source, date and confidence. Avoid black-box intent and unexplained AI scores.

### 4. Change detection
Users care about what changed: new leadership, new job, new technology, redesign, launch, storefront shift, account score movement, reopened opportunity. The timeline should be a signature feature.

### 5. Buyer mapping based on the actual problem
Do not simply return C-level executives. A retention problem may map to Lifecycle/CRM; a technical Shopify problem may map to Ecommerce/Digital/Engineering; agency-owner targets may differ again.

### 6. Commerce-service fit
Map the detected problem to what the workspace actually sells and to approved proof points. The same account should rank differently for a CRO agency versus a retention agency.

### 7. Native operating layer plus integrations
Small teams should be able to use the product without a CRM. Mature teams should be able to sync with their CRM and preserve suppression/history.

### 8. Trust and cost visibility
Where useful, expose provider status, evidence confidence and enrichment cost/credit implications. Never make expensive enrichment feel magical or hidden.

## Common user pain themes to design against
Recent community discussions around GTM/outbound tools repeatedly mention:
- stale or incorrect contact data;
- wasted credits;
- weak/unclear intent signals;
- deliverability worries;
- cumbersome workflow/campaign state;
- reporting that does not answer what to do next;
- too much tooling and too little relevance.

Treat these as product hypotheses to validate, not absolute facts.

## Product thesis
We do not win by sending more cold email. We win by helping commerce sellers identify **fewer, higher-confidence opportunities** and move from evidence to action faster.
